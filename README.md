# WAR: 2125

War 2125 is an ascii survival rogue-like. You are a survivor after a virus break out called the muman virus ruins governments and makes earth abandoned from the galtic, stranding humanity and some alien colonist trapped together.

# About Game Plan
War 2125 is open source mostly because I'm a solo dev who's not looking to sell it on steam. The Main goal for version 0.A is to have a basic loop of raiding cities to gain supplies and weapons with a cddas style crafting system and a whole ton of cbms to use for the endgame. The goal is simple, survive the onslaught of a ton of creatures left here while gaining knowledge on what happened and trying to survive. CBMs will be used for hacking. 

# 0.A plans
0.A is the first version planned for the game, it won't have a lot compared to my end idea, its goal is a simple loop of raiding cities and labs to survive while trying to find the source of the infection with minor boss enemies in the works. Minor music tracks are planned and there well be sound effects in this versions from the start hopefully. The Relase date is planned for Dec 31 - Jan 4th,
